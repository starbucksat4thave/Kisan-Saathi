# NASA Earth Observations & Remote Sensing Datasets

The following satellite missions and geophysical products power Kisan Saathi's barani wheat roadmap:

| Product Short Name | Full Name / Sensor | Resolution | Latency | Application in Kisan Saathi |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SPL4SMGP** (v8) | SMAP L4 Global 3-hourly 9 km EASE-Grid Surface and Root-Zone Soil Moisture | 9 km / 3-hourly | ~2.5 days | Soil Wattar evaluation for wheat sowing & urea absorption |
| **GPM_3IMERGDF** (v07) | Integrated Multi-satellitE Retrievals for GPM (Daily Final & Early Run) | 0.1° (~10 km) / Daily | 4h (Early), 3.5mo (Final) | Rainfall monitoring, dry spell identification, and harvest protection |
| **HLSL30 / HLSS30** (v2.0) | Harmonized Landsat Sentinel-2 Surface Reflectance | 30 m / 2–3 days | ~2 days | High-resolution field NDVI/NDMI vegetation vigor & maturity detection |
| **NASA POWER** (v2.0) | Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources (Agroclimatology API) | 0.5° x 0.5° point | Daily | Tmax, Tmin, GDD (base 0°C), solar radiation, wind speed for spray window |
| **ECO_L2T_LSTE** (v002) | ECOSTRESS Land Surface Temperature & Emissivity | 70 m / variable | ~1 day | Field-scale microclimate heat island & canopy thermal stress |
| **NASADEM_HGT** (v001) | NASA Shuttle Radar Topography Mission Global DEM | 30 m / static | Static | Field slope calculation and summer monsoon soil erosion hazard |
| **Open-Meteo API** | Non-NASA Gap Filler (ECMWF & GFS multi-model ensemble) | 1 km interpolated | Real-time | 7-day hyper-local precipitation probability, wind speed, and humidity |
